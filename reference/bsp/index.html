Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP)

Reference entry · last updated 20260910

Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) is the central bank of the Philippines. Its primary objective is price stability conducive to balanced and sustainable economic growth.[1]

1. First principles: central banking

A central bank manages a country’s monetary system. BSP formulates monetary policy, issues Philippine currency, manages international reserves, and supervises banks and certain non-bank financial institutions.[1]

2. Role in payments

BSP oversees Philippine payment and settlement systems. The National Retail Payment System (NRPS) sets the framework for retail payments between participating institutions.[2]

BSP also operates PhilPaSSplus, the peso settlement system. Participating institutions use central-bank accounts to discharge payment obligations. This institutional settlement is distinct from the balance shown in a customer’s banking app.[3]

3. Retail payment context

InstaPay and PESONet are retail payment arrangements under NRPS.[2]
