National Retail Payment System (NRPS)

Reference entry · last updated 20260910

The National Retail Payment System (NRPS) is the Philippine framework for interoperable retail payments. It establishes common arrangements through which participating institutions exchange payments.[1]

1. First principles: interoperability

Interoperability allows customers of different participating providers to transact with each other. Common rules govern the exchange of instructions and the resulting obligations.[1]

2. Automated clearing houses

An automated clearing house (ACH) under NRPS is a multilateral agreement for a payment stream. It governs clearing and settlement. InstaPay and PESONet serve different transfer needs within this framework.[1]

The US ACH network is a separate system.

3. Governance and operation

BSP oversees the framework. A payment system management body coordinates industry governance. ACH participants agree payment-stream rules, while a clearing switch operator supplies the infrastructure for processing instructions.[1]
